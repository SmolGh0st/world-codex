Summary

The seat of the crown and the home of the Eros royal family, at the very center of the kingdom.

Feel

First made in a sprawling savannah plains, the capital city is often a bustling hub of activity. Always loud and active, only going quieter once curfew hits. More hot temperature typically, and very humid air. Always crowded by people from all races and places, with never a dull moment.

History

Was the first city when The Great Hero took the territory two centuries ago. Since then, the kingdom has expanded greatly, reaching all the way to the sea and mountains, and have certified themselves as one of the more major kingdoms of the world, and the main human kingdom. The capital city itself is a bustling hub of trade, adventure, commerce, diplomacy, and anything else that you can think of.

An assassination attempt was made on royals of this city while they were visiting
Vellhaven, causing Arcy to join the Assassin's Guild later.

Who Lives Here

Prince Lorian Eros - 3rd Royal Prince of the kingdom

Story Hooks

  • Whoever paid for the Vellhaven attempt is more likely to be here than out in the Outskirts.
  • The Assassin's Guild takes hits on anyone for the right price. The most expensive targets in the kingdom are mostly in this city.
  • It is the obvious destination for the "grand adventure" Arcy has wanted her whole life, and the obvious place for her two lives to collide in public.
