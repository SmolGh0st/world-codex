Summary

The Ivory family's farm, out on the thin edge of the kingdom where the worked
land gives out. It has fed the family for generations and is currently being run
almost single-handedly by Arcy Ivory - her father's illness came on suddenly
and her three younger siblings are too young to carry the work.

An hour's walk from Vellhaven, which is close enough to sell a cart of
produce and be home the same day, and close enough to be somewhere else at night
and still make the morning's work.

Nothing about the place is remarkable. That is the point of it. It is what Arcy
took the Guild's money to keep, and it is the reason she can never leave for
long.

Feel

Everything here is a chore that repeats. The ground is workable but tired, and it
takes more out of you each season than it did the last. Sound carries a long way
because there's nothing to stop it - you hear a cart on the road well before you
see it, which is useful if you have reasons to be seen coming home late.

Story Hooks

  • The farm cannot survive Arcy leaving for any length of time. Every adventure has a clock on it.
  • Guild money is paying for something here. Someone will eventually ask where a farmer's daughter got it.
  • Her father's illness has no cause yet. It could be nothing, or it could be the first thing that was done to this family on purpose.
