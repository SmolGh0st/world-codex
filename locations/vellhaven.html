Summary

The nearest town to Ivory Farm and the last real stop on the road before
Amphora. Big enough to have a market, an inn and a reason for royals to
pass through; small enough that a face gets remembered.

This is where Arcy Ivory was caught up in the assassination attempt on
visiting royals - she was in the wrong part of town on the wrong day, ran with
the killers rather than be taken for one, and ended up recruited. The
Assassin's Guild keeps The Basement under the town, which means Arcy's
two lives are an hour apart.

Vellhaven matters because it is the hinge. Everything ordinary is west of it and
everything dangerous is east.

Feel

A working town rather than a pretty one. The market is loud in the morning and
dead by mid-afternoon. Because it sits on the road to the capital it gets a
steady traffic of people who don't live there, which is exactly why the Guild
can operate in it - a stranger is not automatically interesting here.

History

The assassination attempt on the visiting royals happened here. Whether the town
recovered quietly or is still under scrutiny is undecided, and it changes a lot.

Story Hooks

  • Someone in Vellhaven saw Arcy run with the assassins and has not said so yet.
  • The crown may still have people asking questions about the attempt.
  • A town on the capital road sees everything that passes. Whoever runs the inn knows more than anyone with a title does.
