Summary

A cellar beneath an unremarkable building in Vellhaven, used by the
Assassin's Guild as a meeting point, a place to hand over payment, and
somewhere to wait out a bad night.

It is deliberately not a headquarters - one safehouse among an unknown
number. Losing it would cost the Guild a room, not an organisation.

Where Arcy Ivory was trained, and where she goes when she is not being a
farmer.

Feel

Low ceiling, no windows, one way in that anyone would find and at least one that
they wouldn't. It is dry, which is the most anyone will say in its favour. The
building above it is chosen for being boring - the point of the place is that
nobody has a reason to describe it.

Who Lives Here

Nobody lives here. People wait here.

  • Arcy Ivory - trained here; comes at night

Story Hooks

  • If it is compromised, the Guild abandons it without sentiment. Anyone who thought of it as home learns something about the Guild that day.
  • Payment changes hands here, which means the ledger of who hired whom is here too, at least for a while.
  • Arcy has to get from the farm to here and back before morning, routinely. The route is a weak point and someone could learn it.
