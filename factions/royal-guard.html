What They Want

Their goal is to protect the kingdom, civilians, and the royal family.

How They Operate

They have a couple thousand members. Spread out across the whole kingdom, they keep watch over the whole kingdom's territories, spread out over many different posts, but centered in the capital. Long time rivalry with Assassin's Guild, always chasing after their tail but never catching them. Classic royal guard.

History

First founded by the first king's bodyguards, then expanded as they hired more and more people. It started in just Amphora- but soon expanded across the cities and territory.
