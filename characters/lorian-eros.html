Summary

The third royal prince. The brashest and most outgoing prince, he enjoys swordsmanship and the outside world/nature, A love interest for Silver, he was the first to drag her to the capital when she saved him from a chimera in the forest.

Appearance

Tall, brown haired, distinguished looking. Messy short brown hair, with young, energetic eyes. Wears very adventurer-eques outfits, though more fancy and royal. Decently strong build hidden under his clothes.

Personality

A very outside boy. Likes nature and hates being trapped inside. Enjoys getting dirty, and often practices his swordsmanship outside with the guards and soldiers. Very people person, very extroverted. Doesn't expect to have to take the crown being the third prince, he just enjoys his youth to its fullest.

Backstory

Born as the third prince, with two younger sisters, one older sister, and two older brothers. Being the third prince, he didn't get that much attention, which lead to him going out a lot more. He picked up swordsmanship and hopes to one day become a soldier or general, once he's old enough. Slowly began to explore the forest more and more to help refine his swordsmanship skills, which lead to him eventually meeting Silver.
