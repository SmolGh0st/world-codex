Summary

Was a farmer, now working a double life as a new Assassin's guild member. Father got sick and mother died, so she has to take care of the family mainly on her own. Working as assassin for the money, to take care of her family and get medical care for her father. Goes under the assassin name of Grimm.

Appearance

Slightly past shoulder length, wolf cut, blond with streaks of dark blue dyed hair, with my eyes being a light violet purple. Short, 5'5. Average build, slightly on the lankier side.

Personality

Brash, bold, uncaring of many other people's opinions. Sometimes tries to appear tough and uncaring from the outside, but secretly soft and nice. Clingy and a little possessive to close friends. Somewhat slow and not super smart, very clumsy and air headed. Very independent, doesn't like to be chained down. Always wanted/wants to go on grand adventures, but never got a chance to for a long time. Will do anything to keep her little siblings and family safe.

Backstory

Born into a farming family on the outskirts of the kingdom, Arcy Ivory always wanted to be more than just her family legacy. However, she's chained down by her father's sudden illness, needing to help out and farm all day to provide for her 2 little sisters and 1 little brother. When she finally gets the opportunity to go out into the world, she takes it. Arcy accidentally gets caught up in a assassination attempt when royals are visiting the town. She has to run with the real assassins, at first distrusting of them. When they see that she can fight, they try to enlist her to join them, the Assassins Guild. She initially refuses, but ends up joining when seeing how much money they could offer her and her family, going by Grimm as her code name. She now works as a farmer by day, assassin by night.
