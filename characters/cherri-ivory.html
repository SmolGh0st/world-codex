Summary

Arcy's youngest sister and the youngest of all 4 siblings. Didn't know their mother. Enjoys nature, but is physically weak so she gets sick often. Likes taking care of hurt weaker animals. Follows Arcy around and interferes with her double life.

Appearance

Long golden-blond hair, and a short, stubby body. Quite sensitive and weak physically, with pale, light skin. Tends to get sick quite often due to this fact. Wide, curious eyes.

Personality

Loves to explore and go out into the farm field and woods, but often gets lost or sick while she's out there. Trusts Arcy with everything and likes to follow her around, which interferes with Arcy's double life. Is a full on pacifist, and likes taking care of small, weaker animals and such. Restricted to her home and her bed quite often because of illness, which she gets sad about sometimes, though she gets over it quite fast typically.

Backstory

The last born of Arcy's family before her mother passed, she's the youngest child. Just nine years old, she's doted on and cared for by all of Vellhaven when she goes out, which she does whenever she gets the chance. She didn't know her mother that well, like her siblings did, which makes her sad and wants to learn more about her.
